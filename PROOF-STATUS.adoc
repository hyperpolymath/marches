[abstract]
The proof-status file the estate proof policy asks of every home repository
(`hyperpolymath/standards` `3-practice/PROOF-POLICY.adoc` §2 and §8.5). It
records, per theorem group, each witness leg with its route, status token
(§5), correspondence state (§2.2) and receipt, and the Proof Witness Grade
derived from them. It claims nothing the receipts below do not show.

== Classification

marches is *theory* under policy §1: its statements (disciplines, region
DAGs, cuts, path grades) would be true of any implementation and name no
type, function or wire format of one system. Theory has *Agda* as its source
of truth, rechecked in *Rocq* and *Lean* (§2), with no Mathlib.

marches was written in Lean 4 from 2026-07, before the 2026-10-26 ruling.
By §3 its Lean files keep their value but are *target-only evidence*: they
count as the Lean leg only once an Agda proof of the same statement is
`PROVEN` and the two statements have been compared and the comparison
recorded (`RECONCILED`). Until then every Agda row is `OPEN`.

== Theorem groups

`PROVEN-WITH-AXIOMS` means it rests on Lean's built-in `propext` and/or
`Quot.sound`, named in the row. The policy does not yet rule on whether
those two built-ins count as "necessary axioms" (§5,
`TRUSTED-BASE-REDUCTION-POLICY.adoc`); this file takes the cautious reading
and names them. The constructor lemmas Lean generates for structures and
inductives (`*.injEq`) use `propext` in every module that has them. They
are not headline theorems and no row below depends on them.

== Plan: closing the Agda gap

Owner decision 2026-11-09: record the gap here now; the Agda port is the
next body of work.

. *Agda source.* Port the groups above to Agda in this repository
(`--safe --without-K`, no postulates, the §2.1 canonical invocation), group
by group in ROADMAP order. Each group moves from `OPEN` to `PROVEN` with an
Agda receipt; that is `PWG-3`.
. *Lean leg.* Compare each Lean statement with its Agda statement and record
the comparison here. The existing Lean file is then the `re-proved` Lean
leg, `RECONCILED`. It counts towards `PWG-2i` only when, in addition, each
axiom it rests on is admissible under disposition (c) of
`TRUSTED-BASE-REDUCTION-POLICY.adoc` (§8.4, "axioms travel with the
grade"). For the `PROVEN-WITH-AXIOMS` groups that means `propext`, and
`Quot.sound` for the tropical adapter, each with its `docs/proof-debt.md`
entry, or a standards ruling on Lean's built-in axioms. A `PROVEN` group
needs no such step.
. *Rocq leg.* Dedukti transport first, hand re-proof as fallback (§2): this
is `PWG-2ii`, under the same axiom rule (`Print Assumptions`).
. *Receipts.* A pinned headless checker run per commit for each witness,
recording the fields §8.2 lists (checker and version, invocation, input
digest, result, axioms, full commit): `PWG-1`. For the Lean leg this is a
CI job around `lake build` and the axiom census; it is `OPEN`.

Until step 1 lands for a group, nothing in this repository may be called
graded. `PROVEN` and `PROVEN-WITH-AXIOMS` in `README.adoc` mean "accepted by
the Lean kernel", without or with the named axioms, not "discharged under
the estate policy".
